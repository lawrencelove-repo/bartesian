The Lemon Drop is as tasty as its candy namesake but with the added bonus of vodka. This sweet and sour lemon cocktail is perfectly complemented with a sugar rim and lemon for a delicious dessert cocktail. 

Base Spirit: Vodka

Recommended Glassware: Lowball Glass

Garnish: Lemon

Alcohol Volume: Mocktail = 0 oz., Light = 1 oz., Regular = 1.5 oz., Strong = 2.5 oz.
